About This Research

This research compares and evaluates different chatbot techniques to determine their performance in question-answering about everything related to University of Hull's MSc Artificial Intelligence online program. The reason why we examine RAG in comparison to retrieval-only methods is because it is necessary for organizations to have evidence within relevance, coherence, handling ambiguity, and user satisfaction, to justify RAG logic's added cost and complexity compared to the more straightforward, retrieval-only logic.

Research Questions

  • Does Retrieval Augmentation Generation (RAG) chatbot reduce hallucinations compared to generative models without retrieval augmentation mechanisms?
  • Does the Retrieval-Based Chatbot using Embeddings and Cosine Similarity provide more accurate and relevant responses compared to RAG Chatbot?
  • Does the RAG Chatbot responses are more coherent compared to Retrieval-Base Chatbot, when handling open-ended questions?
  • Which method between RAG or Retrieval-Based, better in handling ambiguous/incomplete queries, and more contextually appropriate responses?
  • Do users report higher overall satisfaction with RAG Chatbot because of generate more natural and contextual answers, compared to the Retrieval-Based Chatbot, which focuses on factual accuracy?
  • Does Federated RAG deliver higher accuracy than centralised RAG?
