=== Keele Online – MSc Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence ===

1. Overview
- Mode & Duration: 100 % online, part-time over 2 years (180 credits)
- Start Dates: Six intakes per year (next intake: 2 September 2025; application deadline: 19 August 2025; documents by 26 August 2025)
- Fees: Total £7,440; per 15‑credit module £620; final dissertation instalment £1,860; 10 % Keele alumni discount (£6,696)
- Payment Options: Per‑module or upfront; UK/EU students may apply for government postgraduate loans; fees reviewed annually

2. Entry Requirements
- Academic:
  • A 2:2 UK undergraduate degree (or international equivalent), OR
  • Third‑class honours / ordinary degree plus ≥2 years relevant work experience, OR
  • Non‑degree applicants: ≥3 years relevant computing/IT/data management experience
- English: If English isn’t first language, need one of:
  • IELTS 6.0 (min 5.5 each), TOEFL 79, PTE 50, Cambridge 169 (min 162 each), Duolingo 95 (min 85 each), OR
  • Degree taught in English within the last 5 years

3. Technical Requirements
- Desktop/laptop (Windows/Mac/Linux) with admin rights
- Microsoft Office (Word, Excel) and PDF reader
- Platform: Canvas LMS‑compatible browser
- Additional software per module:
  • Anaconda (programming)
  • XAMPP (web technologies)
  • WEKA (data analytics)

4. Curriculum & Modules
- Core Modules:
  • Applications of AI & Machine Learning
  • Mathematics for AI & Data Science
  • Statistical Data Analytics & Databases
  • Design & Programming (algorithms, data structures, OOP)
  • User Interaction Design
  • Software Engineering with Group Project
  • Web Technologies & Security
  • Research & Consultancy Skills
  • MSc Project phases: Literature Review and Final Project
- Languages & Tools: Python, R, Matlab, XML
- Learning Outcomes:
  • Understand and apply AI techniques (image/speech/data mining/social media analytics)
  • Design, build, deploy, evaluate AI systems and secure web applications

5. Learning Experience & Support
- Interactive online environment: multimedia lectures, peer collaboration, forum discussions
- Dedicated Student Success Coordinator for guidance throughout
- Evidence‑based teaching from research‑active academics (80 % rated world‑leading/internationally excellent in REF 2021)
- Consistently high student satisfaction and employability (top‑10 student experience; 96 % graduate employability per HESA)

6. Career Prospects & Industry Application
- Prepares graduates for roles in AI‑driven sectors: automated customer service, image/speech processing, AI‑based analytics, intelligent systems in logistics/factories/online platforms
- Course informed by industry collaboration (e.g. Knowledge Transfer Partnership with Bentley Motors)
