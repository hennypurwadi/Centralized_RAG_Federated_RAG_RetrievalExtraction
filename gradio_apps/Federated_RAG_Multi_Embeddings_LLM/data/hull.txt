
University of Hull – MSc in Artificial Intelligence (Online) Knowledge Base
============================================================================

Programme Overview
------------------
- Mode & Duration: Fully online, part-time over two years, designed for working professionals.
- Start Dates & Fees:
  - Starts: January, May, September (next cohort starts 22 Sep 2025; deadline: 12 Sep 2025).
  - Fee total: £8,950, plus a £350 acceptance fee (included in total), with payment via instalments.
  - VAT or GST may apply depending on student residence.
- Study Load: Approximately 20 hours per week.

What You’ll Learn
-----------------
- Core Technical Skills:
  - Master Python AI libraries, algorithm implementation.
  - Apply machine learning, deep learning, business analytics, and data visualisation.
- Ethics, Governance & Society:
  - Address fairness, bias, legal standards, misinformation, and emerging AI laws.
- Business & Communication Skills:
  - Develop communication for technical and non-technical audiences, teamwork, stakeholder engagement.
- Research & Consultancy Skills:
  - Research and consultancy projects solving real-world problems across domains (healthcare, legal, climate, etc.).

Course Modules & Structure (180 Credits)
----------------------------------------
1. Artificial Intelligence Foundations (30 credits) – AI concepts, Python, software principles.
2. Machine Learning & Deep Learning (30 credits) – Algorithms, supervised/unsupervised ML, neural networks.
3. Ethical, Regulatory & Social Aspects for Fair AI (30 credits) – Legal, ethical, and social frameworks.
4. Applied Artificial Intelligence (30 credits) – Business-focused AI integration, HCI, communication, misinformation.
5. Research/Consultancy Project (60 credits) – Capstone project tied to workplace or real-world challenge.

Assessment & Feedback
---------------------
- Coursework-only, 100% online (no exams).
- Methods include coding assignments, essays, research reports, presentations, peer/self-reflection, and tutor feedback.

Entry Requirements
------------------
- UK Honours degree (minimum 2:2) in STEM or relevant field with maths/stats/computing element.
- Equivalent professional experience (including coding) considered.
- English Language: IELTS 6.0 overall (no skill below 5.5) or equivalent.

Funding & Payment Plans
-----------------------
- Tuition Fee: £8,950 total (includes £350 acceptance fee).
- Payment Options: Full payment upfront or instalments (typically six payments).
- Funding: UK postgraduate loans, bursaries (~£400), referral discounts.
- Additional Costs: Some textbooks not covered by library subscriptions.

---------------------------
- High-demand roles: AI Researcher, ML/DL Engineer, Data Scientist, NLP Specialist, Python Developer, Robotics Engineer, UX Software Engineer.
- Job Growth: Computer occupations projected to grow 11.7% (2023–33).
- Salary Premium: AI-skilled professionals earn ~17.7% more globally, up to 25% in some markets; UK average premium is 14%.
- Career Preparation: Capstone project builds portfolio; access to Hull’s Careers & Employability service and global alumni network.
